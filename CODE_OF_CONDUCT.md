# Code of conduct

This holds in the issues, the pull requests, the discussions and every other place of the
project.

## What is expected

- Talk about the work: the IL, the evidence for a name, the output of a check. A claim comes
  with what shows it, and "this is an inference" is a fine thing to say.
- Be patient with people who arrive from the game and not from reverse engineering, and with
  people who write in a second language.
- Take a refusal as one. A pull request that cannot be reproduced, or that the rules of
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) exclude, is closed without it being about its author.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, and attacks on a person for who they are.
- Publishing someone's private information, an account name tied to a real identity
  included.
- Hostility toward the game's publisher, its staff or its players. The project studies their
  work; it has no quarrel with them.
- Posting what the repository must not hold: code from `src/`, game files, material from
  leaked source code, cheats or ways around the anti-cheat ([`LEGAL.md`](LEGAL.md)). This is
  removed on sight, whatever the intent.

## What happens

The maintainer edits or removes what breaks these rules and says why. Someone who keeps at
it, or who posts leaked material or cheating tools once, is blocked from the repository.

To report something, contact the repository's owner through GitHub, or use "Report content"
on the comment. Reports are not made public.
